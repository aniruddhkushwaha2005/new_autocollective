# AutoCollective Development Datasets

## Overview & Disclaimer

The CSV files in this directory (`indian_cars_dataset.csv` and `historical_demand.csv`) are **sample development datasets** modeled on publicly known Indian automotive segment pricing structures, city RTO tiers, depreciation curves, and seasonal demand patterns (2020–2026).

- **License**: MIT / Open Academic Use (for B.Tech final-year demonstration and ML model benchmarking).
- **Important Notice**: These records are **not** live real-time dealership feed quotes or verified third-party market transactions. All predictions and forecasts produced by models trained on these datasets must be presented to users with confidence intervals and development-dataset disclaimers.

## Files

1. `indian_cars_dataset.csv`: 360 vehicle pricing records across 12 popular Indian car models (Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Kia, Honda, Toyota, MG, Skoda, Volkswagen) across 7 cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad).
2. `historical_demand.csv`: 1,080 monthly city-by-model demand and group-formation records spanning 18 months (May 2025 – Oct 2026).
